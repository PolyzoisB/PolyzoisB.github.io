Research profile

Statistical Seismology · Stochastic Modelling · Machine Learning

I am a postdoctoral researcher working at the intersection of statistical seismology, applied statistics and machine learning. My work combines Bayesian inference methods, point-process modeling, anomaly detection, clustering, and deep learning to support forecasting, hazard assessment, and decision-making under uncertainty, with applications primarily in natural hazards and cybersecurity.
